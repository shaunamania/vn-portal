Privacy Policy

GBT VN Team Portal is an internal link directory for the GIGABYTE Vietnam marketing team. This page explains what the portal does with your information when you sign in with Google.

What we receive

When you sign in, Google shares your email address, name and profile picture with the portal. The portal does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or any other Google data.

How it is used

  • Your email address is checked against the team's access list to decide whether you can see the portal.
  • Your name, email and picture are shown in the portal header while you are signed in.

What we store

The portal does not keep a database of visitors. Your sign-in token is held only in your browser tab's session storage and is cleared when you sign out or close the tab. The access list itself is maintained by the portal admin.

Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with anyone. It is used only to control access to this portal.
